## Known Limitations

See `blue_nether_spec.md` → "Known Issues and Remaining Work" section for the full list.
Key gaps identified by gap analysis:

- **Entity AI goals not registered** — all custom entity classes extend the correct parent
  but `registerGoals()` is not overridden. Mobs will be stationary and non-responsive.
- **Portal teleport mechanics** — portal block class is generated but teleport logic is
  stubbed. No custom portal is fully wired.
- **Structure biome placement** — structures lack biome placement constraints. They can
  spawn in any biome, not just their intended ones.
- **Trading/bartering tables** — piglin bartering and villager trading not themed.
- **Maps/Clocks/Compasses** — no custom client-side rendering for map patterns, clock
  behavior, or compass behavior in the Blue Nether.
